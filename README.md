# Cybersecurity Open Courses

Free cybersecurity training courses published as open educational resources.

This repository contains cybersecurity courses covering application security, AI security, cloud security, compliance, threat hunting, secure development, software supply chain security, digital forensics, and related topics.

The project serves as a personal portfolio and open educational resource library developed as part of the Truth Surge initiative.

---

## About

I have spent more than fourteen years developing cybersecurity training, educational content, framework mappings, and secure development resources for engineers, security professionals, and organizations.

Over time, many original works evolved through organizational editing, customization, and collaborative development. This repository provides a place to publish, preserve, and continuously improve openly available cybersecurity educational materials in their original form.

The goal is simple:

- Share useful cybersecurity knowledge.
- Build and preserve open educational resources.
- Continuously improve content over time.
- Support the cybersecurity community.

---

## Intended Audience

These materials may be useful for:

- Security professionals
- Software developers and engineers
- Students and career changers
- Security teams
- Educators and trainers
- Organizations seeking cybersecurity awareness and technical training

---

## Continuous Improvement

Courses are developed iteratively and improved over time.

As templates, graphics, workflows, and supporting materials evolve, existing courses may be revised, expanded, or reorganized.

Early courses may vary in style, depth, and presentation quality. Continuous improvement is a core project objective.
