Narration

CMMC 2.0 verifies cybersecurity implementation for protected information across the Defense Industrial Base.

FCI and CUI drive information-protection, system-scope, and contract decisions.

The three CMMC levels connect increasing requirements and assessment assurance to contract risk, with Level 2 aligned to NIST SP 800-171.

Strong programs integrate governance, engineering, operations, supplier management, evidence, assessment, monitoring, and continuous improvement.
